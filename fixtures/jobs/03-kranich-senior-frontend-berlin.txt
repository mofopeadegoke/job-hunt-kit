Senior Frontend Engineer (React) (m/f/d)
Kranich Mobility GmbH
Berlin, Germany · On-site · Full-time

Who we are
Kranich Mobility builds booking and fleet software for car-sharing and e-scooter operators in
Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Our platform handles more than 2 million rides a month.

Your mission
As a Senior Frontend Engineer in our Operator Tools team, you will lead the frontend of the web
app that operators use to manage their fleets, pricing and maintenance. You will set frontend
standards for the team, mentor two mid-level engineers and work closely with our product
designers and backend engineers.

Your responsibilities
- Lead the architecture of our React and TypeScript operator dashboard
- Build real-time map and fleet views (WebSockets, Mapbox GL)
- Set up and maintain our design system together with design
- Own frontend quality: testing strategy, accessibility and performance
- Mentor engineers and run frontend guild sessions

Your profile
- 5+ years of professional frontend development, with at least 3 years in React and TypeScript
- Experience leading frontend architecture decisions in a product team
- Strong testing experience (Jest, React Testing Library, Cypress or Playwright)
- Experience with real-time data in the browser (WebSockets or similar)
- German at C1 level or above: our team, customers and documentation work in German
- Good English

Important information
- This role is on-site in our Berlin office, five days a week
- You must already have the right to work in the EU. We are unable to offer visa sponsorship
  for this position.

Nice to have
- Mapbox GL or other map libraries
- Next.js
- Experience in mobility or logistics
