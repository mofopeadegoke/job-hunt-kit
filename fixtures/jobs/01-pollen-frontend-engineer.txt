Frontend Engineer
Pollen Analytics
Remote (EMEA) · Full-time

About Pollen
Pollen builds analytics software that helps retail chains understand what sells, where and why.
Over 300 retailers use our dashboards every day. We are a team of 60 spread across Europe, the
Middle East and Africa, and we have been remote since day one.

The role
You will join our Product Experience team of five engineers and one designer. Together you own
the web app our customers use every day: report builder, dashboards and the shared component
library behind them. You will work closely with design and product to ship features, and you
will care about how fast and accessible the app is.

What you'll do
- Build new features in our React and TypeScript web app, from design handoff to release
- Grow and maintain our component library and its Storybook documentation
- Write unit, component and end-to-end tests (Jest, React Testing Library, Playwright)
- Improve performance and Core Web Vitals across the app
- Make sure new work meets WCAG 2.1 AA
- Review code and help teammates through pairing and feedback

What we're looking for
- 3+ years of professional experience building web apps with React and TypeScript
- Experience building or maintaining a component library or design system
- Solid testing habits: unit, component and end-to-end tests
- Working knowledge of web accessibility (WCAG) and web performance
- Comfortable consuming REST APIs
- Clear written communication in English; most of our collaboration is async

Nice to have
- Next.js
- Data visualization libraries (D3, Recharts, Visx)
- GraphQL
- Experience in B2B SaaS

Location and hours
This is a fully remote role. You can work from any country in EMEA where we can hire through
our employer of record. You need at least 4 hours of overlap with 9:00–17:00 CET.

What we offer
- Competitive salary benchmarked to your location
- 25 days of paid holiday plus local public holidays
- Home office budget and a yearly learning budget
- Two team meetups a year (travel and visas covered)

Hiring process
1. 30-minute intro call with our recruiter
2. Take-home task (about 3 hours) and a follow-up conversation
3. Technical interview with two engineers
4. Values chat with the Head of Engineering
